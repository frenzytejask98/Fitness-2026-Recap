    Where do I get these?

    Strava: Settings, My Account, Download or Delete Your Account, Request Your Archive.

    Apple Health: Health app, your picture, Export All Health Data (VO₂ max, resting heart rate, sleep, steps).

    Garmin: Garmin Connect, Activities, Export CSV. Or request your full data at garmin.com/account/datamanagement.

    Whoop: App, Menu, Data Export (resting heart rate, sleep, workouts).

    Polar: account.polar.com, Download your data.

    Google Fit: takeout.google.com, choose Fit (steps).

    Coros, Suunto and others: export workouts as FIT, GPX or TCX and drop them in, or sync to Strava and use that export.
